Frequently asked questions

How does WhatsApp appointment booking work for our clinic?

Patients select their consulting doctor, appointment date, and treatment category on your website. The system opens WhatsApp with a pre-formatted message or triggers our automated bot to confirm the appointment, send calendar invites, and provide clinic directions.

Can we display multiple doctors and consultation hours?

Yes. Each doctor receives a dedicated bio page with their individual OPD timings, speciality credentials, consultation fees, and direct booking slots, making it simple for patients to choose the right specialist.

Yes. We adhere strictly to the National Medical Commission (NMC) regulations and medical advertising ethics in India, focusing on factual qualifications, transparent consultation fees, and verifiable clinical contact details without misleading claims.
