Why Indian boutique hotels must stop relying solely on OTAs

Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) such as MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Agoda, and Booking.com charge between 18% and 25% commission on every room night sold. For an independent resort or luxury homestay generating ₹10 Lakhs in monthly room revenue, this translates to over ₹2 Lakhs to ₹2.5 Lakhs lost to third-party aggregator commissions every single month.

A high-converting direct booking website allows you to offer best-rate guarantees, customized weekend packages, and personalized meal plans directly to guests while capturing their phone numbers and emails for repeat marketing campaigns.

Direct reservations protect your cash flow from OTA payment holding cycles, allowing instant settlement of advance guest deposits straight into your hotel's current bank account via direct UPI, net banking, or debit/credit cards.
